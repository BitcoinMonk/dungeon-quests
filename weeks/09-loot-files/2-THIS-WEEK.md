# Week 9 · Loot Files — This week, on your own

Do these in order. One or two a day. Change `[ ]` to `[x]` when a quest is done.
All the puzzles this week use `loot.txt`. Make a new `.py` file **in this folder** for each one.

---

### 9.1 🧩 Count the hoard
How many items are in the hoard altogether? Check your answer.
- [ ] Done

### 9.2 🧩 Gems
How many gems are in the hoard? Check your answer.
- [ ] Done

### 9.3 🧩 What's it worth?
A gold coin is worth **1** gold. A gem is worth **25** gold. A crown is worth **100** gold. Everything else is junk, worth **0**.
What is the whole hoard worth?
Hint: one `total` variable, one loop, and some `if` / `elif`. Check your answer.
- [ ] Done

### 9.4 🔧 Sort the pile
Make two empty lists: `treasure` and `junk`. Loop over the hoard and put each item into the right list (gold coins, gems and crowns are treasure).
Print how many are in each list. Do the two numbers add up to your answer from 9.1?
- [ ] Done

### 9.5 🛠️ Write your own loot file
Make your own text file, `my_loot.txt`, with at least 15 items of your choice, one per line.
Then in a blank `my_loot.py`, read it and print a report: how many items, and how many of one item you pick.
- [ ] Done
