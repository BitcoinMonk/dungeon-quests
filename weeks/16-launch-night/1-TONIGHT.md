# Week 16 · Launch Night — Tonight's lesson

**Together with your mentor. Tonight, your mentor is the player.**

## Tonight's idea

Your mentor plays your dungeon without any help. You built a game from a blank file. Tonight you see someone play it.

## Steps

### 1. Launch (30 min)
Your mentor plays. **You don't help, and you don't touch the keyboard.** Write down:
- everywhere they got stuck
- anything that broke
- anything that made them laugh

### 2. Bug hunt (20 min)
Your mentor now tries to **break** your game on purpose: weird commands, empty input, going back and forth, typing numbers.
Every crash goes on the list.

### 3. Fix together (30 min)
Pick the worst bug from the list and fix it together. Then the next one.

### 4. Look back (10 min)
Open week 1's `dungeon.py` side by side with your `my_dungeon.py`. In week 1 that file was a mystery.

## Mentor notes
- Play for real and react out loud. This is the reward for 16 weeks of work.
- Try to break it properly (empty Enter, `NORTH`, `take` twice). Finding bugs in your own game is what real programmers do too.
